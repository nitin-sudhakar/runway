    How it works

    Hundreds of signals. One plan made for you.

    Runway reads everything that can knock out power where you live, from the trees over your street to the speed of the wind, and runs it through prediction models trained on years of outages. Then it learns how you live, so the plan it gives you fits your home and not an average one.

    1. 01

      Collect

      Public forecasts, satellite and land data, utility records and live outage feeds come together for your ZIP and refresh every hour.

    2. 02

      Predict

      Models trained on past outages estimate the chance of an outage for each period, how long it may last, and how sure they are.

    3. 03

      Understand you

      Your devices, routines, budget and the people you care for decide what matters. You choose what to share and can change it anytime.

    4. 04

      Plan and guide

      Runway turns all of it into steps timed for you. Watson, built on IBM watsonx.ai, answers your questions and updates the plan as things change.

    Runway is a prototype. Its models and ZIP-level exposure data haven't been validated against real outage records yet.
